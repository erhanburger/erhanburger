# Restaurant reference research

Scope: public menu, navigation and case-study text inspected with the web reader. No competitor orders were placed. The primary Brainy reference and live browser behaviour are covered by the separate technical audit. Site motion, precise typography, colour values, responsive behaviour and load timings were not inferred from text-only sources.

| Reference | What was verified | What to transfer to Er-Han |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Shake Shack UK menu](https://www.shakeshack.co.uk/menu/) | Distinct burger, chicken, sides and hot dog sections. Single, double and triple variants are explicitly named. Cheeseburger copy names addable ingredients. Chicken Bites copy states count and dipping sauce choices. | Provide portions and what can be customised at the point of selection. Let non-burgers retain their identity. Keep copy concise and do not borrow portion, promotion or ingredient claims. |
| [Five Guys UK burger menu](https://www.fiveguys.co.uk/menu/burgers/) | Builds each card around a short ingredient description, photograph and Order Now. Toppings are surfaced before ordering, with named combinations offered separately from creating your own. | Explain the customisation promise up front. A verified default and a clear Edit action reduce decision load. Named presets require actual menu approval. |
| [Honest Burgers menu](https://www.honestburgers.co.uk/menus/) | Separates menu sections by beef, chicken, plant options, sides and sauces. Makes the included accompaniment and location-dependent availability explicit, and links price discovery to local click and collect. | Show included vs optional elements and price deltas before the basket. Keep restaurant availability claims tied to verified rules. |
| [Burger & Lobster menus](https://www.burgerandlobster.com/menus/) | Menu starts by choosing a location. Empty cart exposes Continue Shopping. | Make the next sensible action immediate. Retain the Er-Han menu position as the basket is closed. Avoid adding location selection unless the ordering workflow actually needs it. |
| [Black Bear Burger menu](https://www.blackbearburger.com/restaurant-menus) | Burgers, sides and drinks shortcuts, an explicitly identified featured product, and displayed extra prices beside relevant products. Separate venue/halal menus distinguish availability. | Feature one established product with concise product facts. Keep categories and prices readable while maintaining route to the whole menu. |
| [McDonald's UK menu](https://www.mcdonalds.com/gb/en-gb/menu.htm.html) | Skip controls, useful category labels and distinct sections for bundles, sauces, fries/sides and drinks. The menu links into a separate ordering path. | Navigation should remain fast without animation. Prioritise clear categories, keyboard access and distinguish meal bundles from standalone items. |
| [Burger King UK branch page](https://locations.burgerking.co.uk/london/unit-c5-tottenham-hale-retail-park) and [indexed UK menu](https://www.burgerking.co.uk/menu?producttypeid=39) | The official branch page describes featured products, takeaway and ordering entry points. Indexed menu content lists flame-grilled, chicken, veggie, meal, side, drink and dip categories. Direct menu retrieval returned only a JavaScript shell. Actual live customisation and checkout were not verified. | Put local fulfilment and menu information near the action where verified. Treat the indexed category list as information architecture evidence only. |
| [Wendy's UK menu](https://www.wendys.com/en-gb/menu/our-menu) | Lists a scannable category index separating Biggie Bag Meal Deals, combos, burgers, chicken, veggie, sides and beverages. | Make meal deals independently discoverable without flattening sides and drinks into burger categories. |
| [Yeah! Burger on Behance](https://www.behance.net/gallery/15359439/Yeah-Burger) | Designer describes a deliberately playful identity that makes ingredient provenance approachable. This is the case-study rationale, not a live ordering interface audit. | A distinctive wordmark, title rhythm and food-led copy can express warmth. Keep choice labels, pricing and warning text clear. |
| [SMASZ by REELM](https://reelm.pl/en/work/smasz) | Independent studio case study describes a cinematic hero, animated ingredients, WebGPU/Canvas, display typography and a separate practical route to menu and booking. This technology is reported by its creator. Runtime and performance were not tested here. | Separate a signature visual interaction from essential discovery and purchase tasks. Limit immersive motion to meaningful actions. |

[Awwwards' food directory](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/food-drink/) and [Crav Burgers case link](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/crav-burgers) timed out in the web reader. An alternative archive was payment-gated. These examples were not assessed.

## Design and technical principles for the comparison

- Use one dominant product image or preparation view per step. Standardise the size of menu image wells and source-photo crops so product comparison does not require relearning visual proportions.
- Treat display typography as brand expression and use a distinct highly legible text style for portions, category labels, prices and basket information. Colour communicates selection together with text and state.
- Keep category navigation and a clear purchase action within reach on mobile. Use progressive disclosure for optional extras and show included, required and paid choices unambiguously.
- Preserve ingredient layering but let visualisation follow the actual product. A plate or open roll is not a burger tower. Use verified portion markings and illustrative framing where exact weights are not supplied.
- Measure performance after implementation, using a realistic mobile viewport and a throttled cold load. Compare layout stability, initial image bytes, route responsiveness and reduced-motion behaviour. Treat 3D and WebGPU as options for proven benefit, since conventional photography and layered motion can meet interaction goals with a smaller initial payload.
