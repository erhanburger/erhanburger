# Chicken Shish independent audit

## Release 1d8594d

The customer supplied iPhone captures of the Cloudflare menu and the settled Medium and Large with Chips scenes. They show that the existing Shish implementation reached the preview domain. The capture does not contain the deployed commit identifier. The separate card compositions remain the visual minimum and are excluded from replacement.

The settled builder has widely separated, oversized chicken sprites, distinct onion and pepper heaps, and excessive exposed box floor. The silhouette and shadows do not match the compact and interleaved dish in the card. At the same options, changing from six to nine equal-scale sprites verifies a renderer rule, not an accurate visual portion. The user's request for 50% more chicken in Large remains in force. A chip weight or volume has yet to be documented.

### Correction experiment

Test a continuous photographic food composition before adding interactions. The full scene gives a single lens, consistent inter-ingredient shadows and a shared surface. It is evaluated as a visual candidate. A flat photo cannot independently remove cooked onion or peppers, update salad and sauces or animate an opening. Those customer states need registered photographic layers or matched photographic states. The rejected sprite method will not be treated as a release fallback.

### Reference needed for a defensible release

Provide original downloadable files available to all reviewers, including the confirmed portion of chips for Medium and Large (or confirmation of identical portion), and four labelled finished boxes photographed at a fixed angle. A viable way to reproduce the exact visual transitions is a locked-camera sequence: clean closed/open box, chips-only base, grilled chicken after plating, onion/pepper omission states, salad optionally beside, sauce applications and pitta. Shared contact shadows should be present in the real frames. Provide stage images as separate downloadable files, ideally with setup and exposure fixed. The goal is to retain the current static menu cards and match them with the same product and customer selections in the builder.

### Correction experiment verdict

The independent Food Photography session reviewed the photographic candidate. Photorealism 9, reference fidelity 7, appetite appeal 9, HP10 appearance 8, cut and texture 7, placement 9, camera 9, lighting and shadows 9. Scorable mean 8.38 across eight criteria. Portion, menu-to-builder consistency, animation and mobile visual quality were unavailable because the candidate is one still. The slices are too narrow and uniform beside the documentary griddle reference. The candidate is vetoed. The stationary composition improves the shadows and interleaving and has no validated interactive behaviour. There is no replacement or after-implementation browser capture. No changes to the customer-facing assets, UI or published branch were made.

The captured before images and rejected candidate are retained in the shared workspace outside the published branch.
