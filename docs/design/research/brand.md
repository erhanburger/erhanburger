# Brand research

Researched 26 November 2026 by the Creative Direction / Brand agent in Chromium (1440×900 and iPhone 13), with computed fonts and colours from the live pages.

**Not reachable:** mcdonalds.com, awwwards.com, behance.net, dirtyburger.com, bleeckerburger.co.uk, patty-and-bun.co.uk. **Partial:** Black Bear hero video did not load; Burger King images blurred; Shake Shack home mostly carousel and footer; GBK cookie banner covered the hero.

## Per brand

- **Honest Burgers.** Two colours: forest green #1D3012 ground, bone #E8E8E4 panels; everything else is photography. Uppercase display face at 40–44 px with +0.04em tracking for headings, nav and logo; humanist body at 16 px with wide tracking, like a printed menu card. A ruled "scrapbook" grid with thick bone borders, halftone bands and stamp-like boxed nav. Plain, factual voice. Mobile keeps the stamp language and adds a fixed bottom nav.
- **Byron.** Sells a British manner, not a menu ("impossible to eat politely"). An 80 px serif against 13.7 px uppercase labels with +0.14em tracking. Ink navy #030617, warm paper #F0EDE9, one blue accent. Pen illustrations, archive photos, chapter numbering. Mobile bar: Book / Call / Order.
- **Burger & Lobster.** Mischievous voice ("Table manners optional"). Wide geometric display, serif body, script logo. Cream #FBF7EF with green #4A772B buttons and arched colour tabs. Photography of a busy room, not isolated food.
- **Black Bear Burger.** Black ground, one memorable badge mark. Heavy grotesque headlines. Proof stack: press, awards, and an FAQ "Are you halal friendly?". Specific provenance. Weakness: the hero depends on one video and was blank when it failed.
- **GBK.** One saturated yellow plus black, ticket-style bordered boxes used consistently. Geometric uppercase. A cookie banner blocks the hero.
- **Five Guys.** Red, white, black; very heavy uppercase. Hero was a partner promotion: global-chain dilution.
- **Shake Shack.** Line-art icon, friendly geometric type, hand-drawn motifs on cream.
- **Wendy's.** Nostalgia as the asset, but several type systems and a promotional feel.
- **Burger King.** Warm brown #502314 with orange and red, rounded type: one of the more appetising chain palettes.

**Chain versus independent.** Chains lead with promotions, partners and app downloads. Independents sell a point of view: place, year, people, method, voice and visible craft.

## Ten transferable principles

1. Two or three colours at most, each with one job.
2. One characterful display face and one quiet text face; small tracked uppercase for labels.
3. One distinctive mark that works at 24 px (flame, grill bars, stamp), not a stock burger icon.
4. One ownable line, repeated. Voice: specific, wry, British.
5. Lead with provenance: halal, grilled to order, since 2013, Brent South, family-run.
6. Make halal visible near the top and in the footer.
7. Texture sparingly so it feels printed, not templated.
8. Real food on consistent props, light and angle.
9. Mobile actions first: a fixed bar with Menu, Basket and WhatsApp; big touch targets.
10. Never depend on one hero asset or overlay; design the fallback first.

## Three brand directions proposed

- **A. Coal & Cream (charcoal craft).** Grilled to order since 2013, presented like a warm printed menu card. Bebas Neue + Source Serif 4, labels in a tracked grotesque. Charcoal #1B1A18, Bone #F2ECE0, Ember #E2571B, Ash #8C857A, Halal green #2F6B3F (badge only). "Cooked when you order it. Never before."
- **B. North-West London (neighbourhood family).** The family on the Brent South corner, told in chapters from 2013. Fraunces + DM Sans, labels in DM Mono. Navy #14213D, Cream #FAF3E3, Saffron #F2A31B, Brick #B5472E, Ink #1A1A1A. "Family-run since 2013."
- **C. Ticket (WhatsApp-native, bold).** The order is a kitchen ticket: stamps, tick boxes, numbered orders, ruled boxes. Archivo Black / Archivo Narrow, Work Sans, IBM Plex Mono for ticket detail. Paper #EFE8D8, Black #111111, Fire red #D8321C, Mint #1F8A5B (confirm/WhatsApp), Grease yellow #F4C430. "Order's in. Grill's lit."

Facts in these lines (family-run, charcoal, hand-weighed patties) are not verified for Er-Han and must not be used on the site until the owner confirms them. Verified: halal, grilled, fresh, since 2013, Brent South Shopping Park, collection orders via WhatsApp or phone.
