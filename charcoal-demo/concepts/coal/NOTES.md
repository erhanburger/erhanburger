# COAL & CREAM — grilled to order

**Idea.** A printed menu card on a dark counter. Charcoal ground with fine grain, bone type, ruled and double-ruled dividers, tracked caps labels. Ember is used only for actions. A leaf halal stamp is the brand's badge of pride.

**Palette (contrast on charcoal #1B1916).** Charcoal #1B1916, the ground. Counter #12110F, behind the board. Surface #24211D, for cards and wells. Bone #F2ECE0, text (14.9:1). Ash #ABA294, secondary text (7.0:1). Ember #E8622A, actions only (5.2:1; charcoal on ember 5.4:1). Leaf #94CDA0, for confirm and halal only (9.6:1).

**Type.** Big Shoulders Display (condensed, grill-house signage) for names, headlines, prices and tracked labels. Source Serif 4 for body, tile names and the basket. Five sizes.

**Layout.** On phones the builder fills one screen: the title and price sit over the board, tiles scroll underneath, and the order button is pinned below. On desktop the board takes the left 57% at full height and the choices sit in a right-hand column with their own order bar. While you choose, the lid floats above the stack. "Add to basket" drops the lid, puts steam on the burger, turns the button leaf ("Added to basket") and then changes it to "View basket · n items · £". The menu reads like a card, with a quick "+" for items that have no choices.

**Imagery.** One composer (`compose.js`) builds every product from cut-outs, so the builder, menu, deals and hero use the same scale, light and shadow. Meal deals: main centre, chips front left, drink back right (a ghost until chosen). Drinks use the normalised `assets/products/` set.
