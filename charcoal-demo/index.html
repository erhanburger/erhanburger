How ordering works

Three lines. One ticket.

No account and no app to download. Your order goes straight to the grill on WhatsApp.

  1. 01

    Build it

    Tap toppings and sauces. Every tap lands on your food and the price updates as you go.

  2. 02

    Check your ticket

    Your basket prints as a kitchen ticket, exactly as it will be sent. Add your name and a note if you like.

  3. 03

    Send it on WhatsApp

    The ticket is the message. We confirm your collection time on WhatsApp. Prefer to talk? Call 07576 113811.
